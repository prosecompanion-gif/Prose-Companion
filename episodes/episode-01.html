How to Access Your Case Files Online

If you are a party to a case, you have the right to view the documents filed in your case. Every state court system has an online portal where you can look up your case, view filings, check hearing dates, and see what motions have been submitted. The name of the system varies by state — Maryland calls it "Party Access," other states call it Case Search, eCourts, Odyssey, or something else entirely.
